# Coverage-first v3: terminal acquisition result

The registered development stage was invoked once after the isolated capture
lab passed all six boundary probes, both positive controls, ten TLS/authentication
cases, and one marker-checked public IANA scrape. Those engineering results
establish lab readiness, not ranking usefulness.

Development ended **inconclusive during acquisition**. The first three research
queries completed with natural pools of 21, 40, and 40 cards. The fourth produced
20 cards but its arXiv engine failed. The registered hard-failure policy stopped
the remaining nine operations. Nine physical requests were made, below the
39-request reserved worst case and the protocol's 53-request ceiling.

No source capture, Jev transaction, answer generation, or native assessment was
invoked. No Brave call was made. The untouched confirmation stage was not
invoked. Neither ranking quality nor task usefulness was measured; this is
not evidence that coverage-first ranking is ineffective.

## Decision

This invocation is terminal. Retain the raw private receipts and the sanitized
[result](result.json); do not restart, replace pools, regrade, or reuse its
exposed tasks as untouched confirmation. The registration and static-preflight
packet remain unchanged historical evidence.

Coverage-first product implementation remains unqualified under the
[approved release decision](../../../coverage-first-release-decision.md).
Production keeps its existing query-only ranking. Issue #516 remains open;
this result does not complete its production-delivery objective. Any further
scientific execution requires a separately registered study rather than another
invocation of these stage identifiers.

The source, registration, terminal inventory, acquisition summary, and separate
lab qualification are hash-bound in `result.json`. Raw provider responses,
credentials, private endpoints and operational paths remain excluded.
